⏱️ Kifech ta9ra b Pomodoro

Ken t9a3ed 3 swaye3 9oddem l'kteb w ma tfhem chay, l'mochkla mouch fik. L'mochkla fel tari9a.

Chnowa Pomodoro?

Tari9a sahla barcha: ta9ra 25 d9i9a bla ma ta3mel 7atta 7aja o5ra, ba3d ta5edh 5 d9aye9 ra7a. Hedhi esmha "pomodoro" wa7da. Ba3d 4 pomodoros, ta5edh ra7a akbar (15 walla 20 d9i9a).

3lech tekhdem?

  • 25 d9i9a mouch barcha: mokhek ma ykhafech menha, donc tebda as3al.
  • L'ra7a mbarmja: ma t7essech elli 9a3ed "tsajjen" rou7ek, donc ma tkassalch.
  • T7seb l'9raya bel pomodoros: "lyoum 3malt 6" a7sen barcha men "9rit chwaya".

Kifech tebda lyoum

  1. Ekhtar 7aja wa7da bark: mathalan "exercice 3 w 4 physique".
  2. Hezz l'téléphone b3id (walla 7ottou mode avion).
  3. Ch3el timer 25 d9i9a w ebda.
  4. Ki tji fekra o5ra ("lezem nab3ath message…"), ektebha fi warka w kammel.
  5. Ki ysoufer l'timer: 9oum, echrob ma, t7arrek. Ma t7ellech TikTok!
💡 Ken 25 d9i9a barcha 3lik, ebda b 15. Ken 25 9lila, jarreb 45 walla 50. L'mouhem: wa9t ma9tou3 w ra7a ma9tou3a.

Ghaltat yseyy3ou l'pomodoro

  • Tchouf l'téléphone fi wost l'25 d9i9a: hakka l'pomodoro "mat", 3awed men lowel.
  • Ra7a ta5edh 30 d9i9a 3al Insta: l'ra7a 5 d9aye9, mouch episode kemel.
  • Ma t7addedch chnowa bech ta9ra 9bal ma tebda: tseyya3 nos l'wa9t tfakker.
Jarreb Pomodoro m3a s7abek
